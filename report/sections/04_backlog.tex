\item Deterministic simulator and ideal-DR invariance test.
\item Controlled noisy-DR drift experiment.
\item UWB range model and conventional PF fusion baseline.
\item Multi-seed robustness check of the baseline.
\item Unknown-pose annular initialization matching the reference-paper problem.
\item Stationary versus moving auxiliary-node observability/convergence experiment.

\section*{Phase 2--4 outlook}
Phase 2 characterizes update rate, node count, geometry, outages, LOS/NLOS degradation, and DR quality. Phase 3 develops uncertainty/information-aware UWB scheduling and node selection. Phase 4 characterizes DW3000/IMU hardware, measures energy, ports the selected estimator to C/C++, and validates the simulation-to-hardware conclusions.
