\chapter{Research Backlog}
\section{Phase 1 completion criteria}
Phase 1 is complete only after the following sequence is documented and reproducible.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{P1A -- IMU baseline:} deterministic planar IMU mechanization, noisy inertial drift, Gaussian UWB measurement, and conventional PF correction. Current status: implemented and single-seed verification completed.
    \item \textbf{P1B -- robustness of the known-pose baseline:} multi-seed study; sensitivity to initial position/velocity/yaw uncertainty; particle count; IMU noise and bias levels. The purpose is validation, not a large hyperparameter study.
    \item \textbf{P1C -- unknown initial pose:} introduce the global-localization problem. The initial position must satisfy the first UWB range approximately, while initial yaw is unknown. Because the IMU-driven model also contains velocity, the initialization assumptions for speed/velocity must be made explicit.
    \item \textbf{P1D -- geometry/observability:} compare stationary, degenerate, and informative moving auxiliary trajectories; report convergence time, error, particle-cloud evolution, and observability singular values.
    \item \textbf{P1E -- paper reproduction audit:} document each equation and algorithmic step of Han et al.; list every underspecified AACOPF parameter and the chosen interpretation. Distinguish the paper's $\Delta L,\Delta\psi$ DR interface from the thesis IMU-driven mechanization.
    \item \textbf{P1F -- AACOPF implementation:} implement the audited transition/resampling mechanism and compare conventional PF and AACOPF on matched trajectories/noise realizations.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Decision required before unknown-pose implementation}
The next important modeling decision is how closely the paper reproduction and the IMU-driven thesis implementation should be coupled. Two clean options are available:
\begin{description}
    \item[Option A -- two baselines:] reproduce Han et al. with the original $[p_x,p_y,\psi]$ state and $\Delta L,\Delta\psi$ interface, while maintaining the IMU-driven $[p_x,p_y,v_x,v_y,\psi]$ estimator as the hardware-oriented thesis baseline. This is scientifically cleanest for separating reproduction from adaptation.
    \item[Option B -- adapted reproduction:] implement AACOPF directly around the IMU-driven state model. This is closer to the final hardware but makes direct comparison with Han et al. less faithful.
\end{description}
The current recommendation is Option A, followed by a documented transition from the paper baseline to the IMU-driven implementation.

\section{Phase 2--4 outlook}
Phase 2 will vary UWB update rate, node count, geometry, outages, LOS/NLOS degradation, and IMU quality. Phase 3 will develop uncertainty/information-aware UWB scheduling and node selection. Phase 4 will characterize the actual hardware, replace simulated sensor parameters with measured ones, measure energy per UWB transaction, port the selected estimator to C/C++, and compare simulation and hardware.
