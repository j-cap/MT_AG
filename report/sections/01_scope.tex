\chapter{Scope and Research Objective}
The project investigates collaborative localization using inertial/dead-reckoning information and inter-node ultra-wideband (UWB) ranging. The long-term objective is to determine how UWB communication can be scheduled and selected to reduce communication and energy cost while retaining acceptable localization accuracy.

The initial scientific baseline follows the cooperative IMU/UWB formulation of Han et al.~\cite{han2020cooperative}. Their method combines dead reckoning with UWB ranging and investigates localization with as few as one moving auxiliary node. The present project treats reproduction of the baseline as a prerequisite rather than as the thesis contribution.
