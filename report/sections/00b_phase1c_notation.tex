\section*{P1C global-initialization quantities}
The following symbols are introduced by Experiment P1C and complement the general notation tables above.
\begin{longtable}{p{0.30\textwidth}p{0.62\textwidth}}
\toprule
Symbol & Meaning\\
\midrule

\toprule
Symbol & Meaning\\
\midrule
\endhead
$N_{\theta}$ & Number of initial target-position bearing hypotheses distributed around the first-UWB-range ring.\\
$N_{\psi}$ & Number of initial yaw hypotheses distributed over $[-\pi,\pi)$ in the unknown-yaw initialization.\\
$\theta_n$ & Bearing angle of initial position hypothesis $n$ around the known auxiliary position $\bm p_{A,0}$.\\
$\psi_m$ & Initial yaw hypothesis indexed by $m$ in the structured global-pose grid.\\
$n$ & P1C position-bearing hypothesis index when used in $\theta_n$; context distinguishes it from the navigation-frame superscript $(\cdot)^n$.\\
$m$ & P1C yaw-hypothesis index.\\
$r_0^{(n,m)}$ & Initial radial distance from auxiliary node $A$ to the target-position hypothesis associated with bearing $n$ and yaw $m$.\\
$\epsilon_r^{(n,m)}$ & Sampled radial perturbation applied to the first measured range when constructing initial particle $(n,m)$.\\
$\sigma_r$ & Standard deviation of the radial initialization perturbation; set equal to $\sigma_{\mathrm{UWB}}$ in the realistic P1C experiments.\\
$s_0$ & Assumed or sampled initial target speed magnitude used to initialize the velocity states.\\
$e_{p,\mathrm{thr}}$ & Diagnostic position-error threshold used to define terminal convergence in P1C; currently \SI{1}{m}.\\
$e_{\psi,\mathrm{thr}}$ & Diagnostic absolute yaw-error threshold used to define terminal pose convergence; currently $10^{\circ}$.\\
$t_{\mathrm{conv}}$ & Earliest time after the final threshold violation such that the relevant convergence conditions remain satisfied until the end of the experiment.\\
$T_{\mathrm{hold}}$ & Minimum remaining duration required after $t_{\mathrm{conv}}$ for a run to count as converged; currently \SI{5}{s}.\\
$T_{\mathrm{late}}$ & Length of the terminal evaluation window used for late-window RMSE; currently \SI{20}{s}.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
